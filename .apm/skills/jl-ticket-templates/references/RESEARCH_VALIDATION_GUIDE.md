# Research Validation Guide

Use this guide to validate new Research tickets. Shared fields and lifecycle rules are in `SHARED_BASE_SCHEMA.md`.

## Creation

Require a neutral Investigation Goal, explicit in-scope and out-of-scope boundaries, and at least three checkable criteria.

Do not require Findings, a Recommendation, or a chosen option before work starts.

## Resolution

Require evidence-backed Findings and a Recommendation that links its reasoning
to cited findings and states trade-offs. The user reviews and accepts the
result. A `Done` ticket with a recommendation but no cited finding is invalid.

## Criteria

Use one criterion per checklist item. Give each item a unique, sequential ID that starts at `AC001:`.
