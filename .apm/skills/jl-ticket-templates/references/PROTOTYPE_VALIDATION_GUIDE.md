# Prototype Validation Guide

Use this guide for new Prototype tickets. Shared rules are in `SHARED_BASE_SCHEMA.md`.

## Creation

Require an answerable Research Question and an Implementation Approach.
Time-box the approach and separate what to build from what not to build.
Require three measurable Verification checks and a Throwaway Plan that says
what to discard versus retain. An approach without a throwaway plan is invalid.

Require at least three checkable acceptance criteria. Use one outcome per item and sequential IDs that start at `AC001:`.

## Resolution

Require observed Findings with results for each Verification check linked to
the Research Question. State what was discarded and what findings carry forward.
The user reviews the outcome before closure.
