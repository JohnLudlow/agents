# Prototype Validation Guide

Use this guide for new Prototype tickets. Shared rules are in `SHARED_BASE_SCHEMA.md`.

## Creation

Require an answerable Research Question and an Implementation Approach.
Separate what to build from what not to build.
Require three observable Verification checks and a Throwaway Plan.

Require at least three checkable acceptance criteria. Use one outcome per item and sequential IDs that start at `AC001:`.

## Resolution

Require Findings with verification results linked to the Research Question.
State what to discard and what findings carry forward.
The user reviews the outcome before closure.
